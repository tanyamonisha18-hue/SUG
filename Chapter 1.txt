# Chapter 1: About Spotify

## What Is Spotify?

Spotify is a digital audio streaming service. It allows people to listen to:

- Music
- Podcasts
- Audiobooks

Users can search for songs, artists, albums, playlists, and podcasts. They can also create playlists, save songs, and discover new music.

Spotify can be used on:

- Smartphones
- Computers
- Tablets
- Other supported devices

## When Was Spotify Founded?

Spotify was founded in **April 2006** by **Daniel Ek** and **Martin Lorentzon**.

The company was started in **Sweden**. Spotify was created to provide an easy and legal way for people to listen to music online.

Spotify officially launched its streaming service in **2008**.

## Who Founded Spotify?

Spotify was founded by:

- **Daniel Ek** – Founder
- **Martin Lorentzon** – Co-founder

Daniel Ek and Martin Lorentzon started Spotify with the idea of making music easy to access online.

## Spotify Timeline

| Year | Event |

## What Can You Do on Spotify?

With Spotify, you can:

1. Listen to music.
2. Listen to podcasts.
3. Listen to audiobooks.
4. Search for songs and artists.
5. Create playlists.
6. Save your favorite songs.
7. Follow artists and podcasts.
8. Discover new music.
9. Share music with others.

## Why Do People Use Spotify?

People use Spotify because it provides a large collection of music and other audio content in one place.

Spotify also makes it easy to search for music, create playlists, save favorite songs, and discover new content.
