# Chapter 8: Listening to Podcasts

## What Is a Podcast?

A podcast is a series of audio episodes about a specific topic. Spotify has podcasts about many different subjects.

You can find podcasts about:

- News
- Education
- Sports
- Comedy
- Technology
- Stories
- Health and fitness

## Search for a Podcast

You can use the Search option to find a podcast.

### Steps

1. Open Spotify.
2. Select **Search**.
3. Enter the name of a podcast or topic.
4. Select the podcast from the search results.

## Play a Podcast Episode

After finding a podcast, you can select an episode and start listening.

### Steps

1. Open the podcast.
2. Select an episode.
3. Select **Play**.
4. Use the playback controls to pause, play, or skip the episode.

## Follow a Podcast

You can follow a podcast to find it easily in the future.

### Steps

1. Open the podcast.
2. Select **Follow**.
3. The podcast is added to your library.

## Find Your Podcasts

You can find the podcasts you follow in **Your Library**.

1. Open Spotify.
2. Go to **Your Library**.
3. Select the podcast you want to listen to.
4. Select an episode.
