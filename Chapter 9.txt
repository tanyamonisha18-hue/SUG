# Chapter 9: Discovering New Music

## Discover New Music

Spotify can help you find new songs, artists, and playlists. It recommends music based on what you listen to.

## Explore Recommended Music

You can find recommended music on the **Home** page.

### Steps

1. Open Spotify.
2. Go to the **Home** page.
3. Browse the recommended songs and playlists.
4. Select a song or playlist.
5. Select **Play**.

## Find New Releases

You can find new songs and albums from your favorite artists.

### Steps

1. Open Spotify.
2. Select **Search**.
3. Search for an artist.
4. Open the artist's profile.
5. Check their latest songs and albums.

## Explore Playlists

Spotify has playlists for different moods and activities.

You can find playlists for:

- Workout
- Study
- Travel
- Party
- Relaxation

Select a playlist to view the songs and start listening.

## Discover Similar Artists

If you like an artist, you can explore other artists with similar music.

1. Search for an artist you like.
2. Open the artist's profile.
3. Browse the recommended or related content.
4. Select an artist to explore their music.

## Save New Music

If you find a song you like, you can save it for later.

1. Open the song.
2. Select the **Like** button.
3. Find the song later in **Your Library**.
