# Chapter 6: Playing and Controlling Music

## Play a Song

Spotify allows you to play songs from albums, playlists, and search results.

Follow these steps to play a song:

1. Open Spotify.
2. Search for a song.
3. Select the song.
4. Select **Play**.

The song will start playing.

## Pause a Song

You can pause a song at any time.

1. Open the music player.
2. Select **Pause**.

Select **Play** again to continue listening.

## Skip a Song

You can move to the next song while listening to music.

1. Open the music player.
2. Select **Next**.

The next song will start playing.

## Play the Previous Song

You can return to the previous song.

1. Open the music player.
2. Select **Previous**.

## Change the Volume

You can increase or decrease the volume while listening to music.

1. Find the **Volume** control.
2. Increase the volume to make the music louder.
3. Decrease the volume to make the music quieter.

## Use Shuffle

Shuffle plays songs in a different order.

1. Open a playlist or album.
2. Select the **Shuffle** icon.
3. The songs will play in a shuffled order.

Select the **Shuffle** icon again to turn it off.

## Use Repeat

Repeat allows you to play a song, album, or playlist again.

1. Start playing music.
2. Select the **Repeat** icon.
3. Select it again to change the repeat option.
