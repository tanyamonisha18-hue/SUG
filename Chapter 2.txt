# Chapter 2: Getting Started with Spotify

## What You Need

Before you start using Spotify, you need:

- A smartphone, tablet, or computer
- An internet connection
- A Spotify account
- The Spotify app or a web browser

## Create a Spotify Account

If you do not have a Spotify account, you can create one.

### Steps

1. Open the Spotify app or Spotify website.
2. Select **Sign up**.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Create a password.
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Enter your name.
7. Select **Sign up**.

Your Spotify account is now ready to use.

## Log In to Spotify

If you already have an account, you can log in.

### Steps

1. Open Spotify.
2. Select **Log in**.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your password.
5. Select **Log in**.

## Choose Your Music Preferences

When you start using Spotify, you may see music recommendations based on your interests.

You can search for your favorite:

- Artists
- Songs
- Albums
- Playlists
- Podcasts

Listening to different types of music helps Spotify provide better recommendations.

## Explore the Spotify Home Page

After you log in, the **Home** page shows different music and audio content.

You can find:

- Recommended songs
- Recently played music
- Popular playlists
- New releases
- Podcasts
