# Chapter 7: Saving and Managing Favorite Music

## Save a Song

You can save songs that you like to find them easily later.

### Steps

1. Open Spotify.
2. Find the song you want to save.
3. Select the **Like** button.
4. The song is added to your liked songs.

## Find Liked Songs

You can find the songs you have saved in **Your Library**.

### Steps

1. Open Spotify.
2. Go to **Your Library**.
3. Select **Liked Songs**.
4. Select a song to play it.

## Save an Album

You can save an album to your library.

### Steps

1. Search for an album.
2. Open the album.
3. Select the **Like** or **Save** option.
4. The album is added to your library.

## Follow an Artist

You can follow your favorite artists to keep up with their new music.

### Steps

1. Search for the artist.
2. Open the artist's profile.
3. Select **Follow**.

## Remove a Saved Song

If you no longer want to save a song, you can remove it.

### Steps

1. Open **Your Library**.
2. Open **Liked Songs**.
3. Find the song you want to remove.
4. Select the **Like** button again.
