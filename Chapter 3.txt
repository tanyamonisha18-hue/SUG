# Chapter 3: Using the Spotify Interface

## Spotify Home Page

The Spotify Home page helps you find music and other audio content.

You can use the Home page to:

- Find recommended music.
- Continue listening to recently played songs.
- Find new releases.
- Browse playlists.
- Find podcasts.

## Your Library

**Your Library** stores the music and playlists that you save.

You can use Your Library to find:

- Playlists
- Songs
- Albums
- Artists
- Podcasts

## Search

The **Search** option helps you find specific content.

You can search for:

- Songs
- Artists
- Albums
- Playlists
- Podcasts

### Search for Music

1. Select **Search**.
2. Enter the name of a song or artist.
3. Select a result from the list.
4. Select **Play** to listen.

## Music Player

The music player appears when you play a song.

You can use the music player to:

- Play or pause a song.
- Skip to the next song.
- Go back to the previous song.
- Change the volume.
- Turn Shuffle on or off.
- Turn Repeat on or off.

## Settings

You can use **Settings** to change different Spotify options.

Depending on your device and account, you can change settings such as:

- Audio quality
- Notifications
- Language
- Playback options
- Privacy settings
